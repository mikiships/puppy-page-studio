Before a deposit

Trust should feel like good care, not fine print.

Sale-pending puppies can still be worth asking about. The details sit in the buyer path so nobody has to choose between excitement and caution.

Availability Is the hold final?

Ask whether either sale-pending hold might fall through, or whether the waitlist is the better next step.

Parents Who are mom and dad?

Confirm parent names, photos, adult weights, temperament, and any registry or health context.

Records What comes home?

Ask for vet exam, vaccine, deworming, microchip, registration, and health guarantee details in writing.

Money What are the terms?

Confirm deposit amount, refund rules, payment method, pickup date, and what happens if timing changes.
